# Place a seed's conditional metadata above the genesis narrative

## Ticket

> A seed's conditional metadata lines — `External:`, `Parent:`, `Roadmap brief:`, `Supersedes:` — are written after the genesis narrative, so they land at the very bottom of the file. A narrative is as long as the idea it explains, and many run to several paragraphs, so the fields that answer the quickest questions about a thread — is a ticket linked, does it hang off a roadmap parent, does it supersede something — are the last thing a reader reaches and the easiest to miss when skimming.
>
> These lines also read as a header rather than as a continuation: they name the thread's external and structural relationships instead of carrying the story the narrative tells. Encountering them first frames the narrative, since knowing that a thread came from a ticket or from a roadmap brief changes how its opening paragraph reads.
>
> The wanted outcome is a seed whose applicable metadata sits at the top, directly under the title heading and before the genesis narrative.

## Intent

Using frontmatter fields for the seed's conditional metadata is an option under consideration.
