# Preserve a ticket's own words when a thread is opened from it

`/open-thread` accepts a ticket as a starting point and composes the seed's genesis narrative from that ticket's title and body. Today that narrative is written fresh — the agent reads the ticket and produces its own self-contained account of why the work exists.

That rewrite is where the ticket's wording goes. Whatever its author was careful about — the framing, a constraint phrased a particular way, the sentence that names the trigger — survives only as well as a paraphrase survives it, and from then on the seed's version is what every later artifact in the thread reads. `External:` keeps the ticket one click away, but nothing carries its actual text into the thread.

It is sharpest for tickets `/open-ticket` files, since that skill writes the body specifically so a thread can take it as its genesis narrative unchanged; `/open-thread` then rewrites prose that was composed to be used as-is.

A thread opened from a ticket should end up holding the ticket's own account rather than a re-derivation of it. Two shapes look plausible and neither is picked yet: leaning on the external link alone, or copying the ticket body into the narrative verbatim. Settling that is part of the work.
